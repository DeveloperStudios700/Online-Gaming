About Online Gaming

Online gaming allows players to connect through the internet using computers, mobile devices, tablets and gaming consoles. Millions of people play online games every day.

Key Findings

  • Improves teamwork and communication.
  • Provides entertainment and relaxation.
  • Connects players worldwide.
  • Can develop problem-solving skills.

Risks

  • Cyberbullying.
  • Online scams and fraud.
  • Identity theft.
  • Privacy concerns.
  • Excessive screen time.

Results & Conclusion

Online gaming offers significant benefits when used responsibly. Players should follow online safety practices and maintain a healthy balance between gaming and daily activities.
