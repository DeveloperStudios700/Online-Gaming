Online gaming is one of the most popular online services in the modern world. It allows people to connect, compete, and cooperate through internet-enabled games.

About the Study

This study examines the impact of online gaming on consumers, including its benefits, accessibility, and potential risks. It explores how digital devices and online platforms have transformed entertainment.

Findings

  • Online gaming provides entertainment and social interaction.
  • Players can develop teamwork and problem-solving skills.
  • Gaming communities connect people worldwide.
  • Cybersecurity risks remain a major concern.
